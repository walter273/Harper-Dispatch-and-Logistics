Carrier relationship

Agreement overview

Understand the expected operating workflow before requesting carrier onboarding with Harper Dispatch and Logistics.

This is not a signed agreement

This page is an operational overview only. A company-approved agreement and any fees, terms, or legal notices must be reviewed and accepted separately.

Carrier dispatch offer

5% only after an acceptable load is booked.

  • No onboarding fee
  • No weekly payment upfront
  • 5% only after Harper books an acceptable load
  • No long-term contract
  • First seven days or first load as a trial
  • The carrier approves every load before booking
  • Rate confirmations and broker details remain visible

Fee basis: the 5% service fee applies only to collected line-haul revenue on loads dispatched by Harper and approved by the carrier. Fuel surcharges, detention, TONU, lumper fees and other reimbursements are excluded. Unpaid loads are included only when payment is collected. If no acceptable load is booked and collected, the service fee is $0.

Carrier control: Harper does not book a load without carrier approval. The carrier may decline any load and may end dispatch service without a long-term commitment, subject to payment of any earned fee on previously approved and completed loads.

Trial: the trial ends after the first seven days or the first approved load booked by Harper, whichever occurs first. Continuing afterward requires the company-approved dispatch agreement.

Submitting a carrier request does not create a subscription or charge.
