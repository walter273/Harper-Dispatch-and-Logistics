SOLUTIONS FOR SHIPPERS

Bring freight requests, coordination and delivery records together.

Shipper Control helps shipping teams organize shipment needs and follow the coordination process from request through delivery. It gives your team a shared place to work with recorded freight information and communicate with the participants supporting the move.

Service benefitsPractical exampleGetting started
01 / SERVICE BENEFIT

Make shipment requirements clear from the start

Equipment, appointment and handling details can be missed when a request is spread across emails.

How Harper helps

Bring your origin, destination, freight details and pickup and delivery requirements into an organized shipment request.

Why it matters for your team

The teams reviewing the move have a clearer starting point, which can reduce repeated questions and avoidable misunderstandings.

02 / SERVICE BENEFIT

Coordinate capacity around the actual move

Your shipment needs suitable equipment and an agreed schedule, not just a name on a list.

How Harper helps

Share your capacity needs and coordinate with the parties reviewing the shipment. Keep requirements and confirmations connected to the request.

Why it matters for your team

You can compare the proposed arrangements with your shipping needs. Coverage and appointments still need confirmation; the platform does not guarantee a truck.

03 / SERVICE BENEFIT

Follow recorded shipment milestones

Shipping teams often need to know which loads need a follow-up before they become urgent.

How Harper helps

Review recorded pickup, transit and delivery updates and keep notes about changes or exceptions with the shipment.

Why it matters for your team

You can identify the next question to resolve and communicate internally with more context. Updates reflect the information received, rather than a promise of continuous GPS tracking.

04 / SERVICE BENEFIT

Keep your team aligned through changes

A different person may handle the shipment when an appointment or delivery requirement changes.

How Harper helps

Use the shipment record to keep relevant messages, notes and documents together across handoffs.

Why it matters for your team

Your team can review the history and continue the conversation without asking the customer or carrier to repeat every detail.

05 / SERVICE BENEFIT

Find delivery records when you need them

After delivery, missing or scattered documentation can add work for operations and accounting.

How Harper helps

Organize available shipment documents and proof of delivery with the related load, and identify outstanding items for follow-up.

Why it matters for your team

It is easier to prepare your internal reconciliation and answer later questions about a shipment. Your existing accounting and payment processes remain part of the closeout.
