FROM LANE SEARCH TO APPROVAL

Know what comes next.

  1. Find your fit

    Use origin, destination, and equipment filters in the board to narrow the freight view to your operation.

  2. Review with dispatch

    Confirm the load details, rate, schedule, and requirements. Ask questions before committing your truck.

  3. Approve the load

    You decide whether to proceed. Harper does not book a load without your approval and helps coordinate broker communication and paperwork.

Before you request access.

Are the loads on this page available now?

No. This public page contains illustrative samples only. Confirm current freight availability and final load details with Harper.

Does requesting access create an account or a charge?

No. Your request starts a review. Approved applicants receive an invitation to activate an account; service requires approval and a signed agreement.

Can I use the load board without dispatch services?

Contact Harper to confirm whether load-board-only access is available for your operation and what terms apply. The 5% price above describes carrier dispatch services.

What if I am a broker or shipper?

Visit broker solutions or shipper solutions for the appropriate onboarding path.
